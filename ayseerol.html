About Me

Hello, I am Ayşe, I am an architecture student at Mimar Sinan Fine Arts University. I am student of third class. I am a 2021 Architectural Restoration graduate. Architecture was my dream since childhood and my family thought that I was suitable for this profession. I used to participate in art and knowledge competitions at school. I was a school representative for a while. I received the first prize in philosophy. This year a special guest came to our school, Maria Sol Echeverri. It was very exciting for me to meet a global person who was warm, sincere and humble. It left a hopeful impression on me for the future. While studying restoration in Ayvalik, my interest in historical buildings increased and I had the opportunity to research more. One of my current dreams is to visit buildings abroad, capture that spirit and transfer it to architecture and my future projects.
